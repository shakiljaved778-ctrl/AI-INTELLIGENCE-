Benchmarks can be gamed and press releases spun, but usage is harder to fake.
On OpenRouter — a popular marketplace where developers reach many models through
one interface — Chinese labs have surged to the top of the charts.

## The usage signal

At one point in mid-2026, six of OpenRouter's top ten models — and all of the
top five — came from Chinese companies, including Tencent, Xiaomi, DeepSeek,
MiniMax, Moonshot, and Z.ai. Because those rankings reflect what developers
actually route traffic to, they cut through marketing in a way leaderboard
scores don't.

## Why developers are choosing them

The pull is a familiar mix: strong capability, open weights, and — decisively —
low price. When a Chinese open model matches a US frontier tier on a given task
for a fraction of the cost, cost-sensitive developers route to it, and the usage
charts follow.

## The bigger picture

None of this means Chinese models lead on every hard task; the US frontier labs
still claim the top of many reasoning benchmarks. But on the practical question
of what gets used in production, the center of gravity has shifted more than the
Western narrative often admits. For buyers, it is another reminder to evaluate on
their own workloads — the best fit may not carry a familiar Western name.
