The calendar has done its annual work: the long, 162-game grind of the baseball
season is nearly spent, and the postseason picture is coming into focus. Division
winners are locking in home-field advantage, the wild-card race is down to a
handful of teams trading places by the day, and the sport is settling into the
stretch where every game starts to feel like a playoff game before the playoffs
begin.

Baseball's regular season is a marathon that rewards depth and consistency; its
postseason is a sprint that rewards neither reliably. That mismatch is exactly
what makes October compelling. A team that ground out ninety-plus wins over six
months can be knocked out in a short series by a hot pitcher and a couple of timely
swings — and everyone who follows the game knows it.

## What the standings say

At the top sit a few clubs that separated themselves early and never came back to
the field: deep rotations, balanced lineups, and the luxury of setting up their
pitching for the first round. Behind them, the wild-card scramble is the usual
late-September theater — teams playing meaningful baseball into the final weekend,
a game or two deciding who gets in and who goes home.

Seeding earns you rest and home cooking. What it does not buy is safety. The
first-round format has a long history of humbling favorites, and the teams
clinching now know that the reward for a great season is merely a ticket to the
part where none of it counts.

## What to watch

The early rounds tend to turn on two things: which staffs can line up their best
arms for a short series, and which lineups stay dangerous when the pitching gets
sharper and the margins shrink. Star power helps, but October has always favored
teams that can win a low-scoring game — pitching, defense, and a bullpen that
holds.

For fans, the appeal is simple and durable. The field is nearly set, the races are
tight, and in a few days the season's long arithmetic gives way to baseball's
best trick: throwing six months of results into a short series where anything can
happen. October is almost here.
