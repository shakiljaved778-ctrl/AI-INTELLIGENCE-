JPMorgan Chase has become one of the most aggressive AI deployers in any
industry — and, unusually, it can point to numbers. The bank reportedly runs
**more than 450 agentic AI use cases in production every day**, and posted record
Q2 2026 results with both top- and bottom-line beats.

## From pilot to production

The headline example is longstanding: COIN, the bank's contract-intelligence
platform, is credited with saving on the order of **360,000 lawyer-hours a
year** by parsing agreements that once consumed junior staff. That is the kind
of concrete, repeatable win that turns AI from a line item into leverage.

## Breadth, not just one big bet

What stands out in 2026 is breadth. Rather than a single flagship model, JPMorgan
has spread hundreds of narrow agents across fraud detection, compliance,
operations, and internal knowledge work — each scoped tightly enough to measure.

## The lesson for everyone else

JPMorgan isn't a tech company; it's a bank that treats AI as infrastructure. Its
approach — many narrow, measurable deployments integrated into existing
workflows, with humans handling exceptions — is exactly the pattern the ROI
data rewards. The takeaway for other incumbents: you don't need to build models
to win with AI. You need to deploy them relentlessly where the work actually is.
