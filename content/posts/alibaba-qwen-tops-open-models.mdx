Alibaba's **Qwen** family has, by one widely cited ranking, overtaken Meta and
Google to claim the top position among open models globally — a milestone for a
company better known in the West as an e-commerce giant than an AI lab.

## From retailer to model maker

Alibaba has invested heavily in generative AI, and Qwen has become one of the
most downloaded and fine-tuned open families in the world, spawning a large
ecosystem of derivative models. The company has also pushed into multimodal
generation, with systems that produce text, images, and video from a single
prompt.

## Chasing the reasoning frontier

Alibaba's newer **Qwen3-Max-Thinking** targets the same reasoning-heavy
territory as the top US models, and the company has claimed competitive results
against them. As always, independent evaluation is the real test — but the
ambition is unmistakable.

## Why the ranking matters

Open-model leadership is strategic. The family that developers build on becomes
the default others extend, shaping tooling, standards, and mindshare. Qwen
sitting atop the open rankings means a meaningful share of the world's
open-source AI now traces back to Hangzhou — a shift with commercial and
geopolitical weight.
