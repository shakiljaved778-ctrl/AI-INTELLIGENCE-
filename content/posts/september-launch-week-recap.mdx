Rarely does the AI industry compress this much into seven days. In the first week
of September 2026, the four largest labs each shipped a new frontier model — a
run of launches that will be remembered as a single event.

## The timeline

- **September 1 — Anthropic** opened with Claude Fable 5.1 (and a companion,
  Mythos 5.1), pairing a capability bump with sharp cuts to cached-input pricing.
- **September 2 — Google** followed with Gemini 3.8 Flash, pushing frontier-
  adjacent capability into a cheaper tier.
- **September 3 — OpenAI** launched GPT-6 Astra, staged across tiers, and called
  it a "generational leap."
- **September 4 — Meta** rounded out the week with a new flagship model.

## The mood

By September 6, CNBC had a name for the collective exhaustion: "model fatigue."
IT buyers described re-benchmarking their workloads only to watch the goalposts
move days later.

## Why it mattered

Beyond the individual releases, the week signaled a new tempo. The frontier is no
longer advanced in occasional, spaced-out leaps but in overlapping bursts — a
cadence that rewards teams with standing evaluation processes and punishes those
who try to chase every launch.
